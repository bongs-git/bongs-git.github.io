INDAI Backtest for TradingView

INDAI's setup scanner as a TradingView strategy. The Strategy Tester gives every number over months of real candles, and Bar Replay plays a day forward one candle at a time.

  1. Tap Copy the script. In TradingView (on a computer, or the website in a phone browser) open the Pine Editor, paste it over whatever is there, and press Save, then Add to chart. After that it is under Indicators, My scripts, on every device.
  2. Use a 5-minute chart on regular trading hours. In the strategy's settings set the score line and risk per trade to match your plan.
  3. In INDAI open Ideas, Retrospective, and tap Copy the changes for TradingView. Paste that line into the strategy's Changes setting. Untick Apply the changes to see the same test without them.
  4. The box on the chart shows the main results (trades, win rate, result, profit factor, drawdown), and the Strategy Tester tab lists every trade. For live simulation press Replay, pick a starting candle and play: the strategy alerts and trades as each candle closes, and the box keeps count.
  5. The numbers TradingView prints beside each trade are shares, not dollars: -163 is 163 shares sold short, and +163 with "stop" or "target" is those shares bought back there.
